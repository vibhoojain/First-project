Meet the Founder

Vibhoo Jain

Creator of Career Navigator

Vibhoo Jain is the creator of Career Navigator, a platform built to help students and beginners discover the right career paths and develop the skills needed to succeed in today's digital world.

With a strong interest in technology, digital learning, and career development, Vibhoo focuses on simplifying complex career decisions for students who often struggle to find clear guidance. Career Navigator was created to provide structured learning roadmaps, skill recommendations, and curated resources so that learners can move from confusion to clarity.

The mission behind Career Navigator is simple: make career discovery easier by bringing trusted learning resources, career roadmaps, and real-world guidance together in one place.

Vibhoo believes that the right information at the right time can completely change someone's professional journey. Through this platform, the goal is to help people explore opportunities, build valuable skills, and start earning with confidence.
